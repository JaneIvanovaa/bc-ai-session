Why Start with Copilot?

It's the same brain — but already inside your Microsoft workspace

Copilot runs on the same ChatGPT models and Claude — but works directly inside Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, and SharePoint. You don't switch tools, you don't copy-paste. It works where you already are.

What makes Copilot different
  • Knows your files — ask "Who is responsible for M2M?" and Copilot finds the answer in your internal docs. ChatGPT cannot.
  • Works in your inbox — summarise emails, set out-of-office, find specific emails without copy-pasting.
  • Knows your meetings — get meeting summaries and action points directly in the Teams chat.
  • Secure by default — your data stays in Interfood's Microsoft environment.
ChatGPT vs Copilot
⚠️ Do not use free ChatGPT — only ChatGPT paid (Teams) or Copilot. Free ChatGPT does not meet Interfood's security requirements.

Copilot

✓Access to all Interfood files, emails, Teams & calendar
✓Works inside Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint
✓Knows colleagues & internal processes
✓Uses ChatGPT models + Claude (via Copilot Cowork)

ChatGPT (paid)

✓Deep analysis of uploaded files
✓Strong at research and writing
✗Does NOT know internal Interfood data
✗Cannot work in Outlook or Teams
✓Use as a complement when Copilot falls short
Start with Copilot. If something doesn't work there, check the Use Case Matrix.
